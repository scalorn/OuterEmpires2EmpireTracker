NYRIDION EXPANSION CONSORTIUM LAUNCHES THE KEYSTONE-CLASS STRATEGIC MOVER — NEXT-GENERATION LOGISTICS AND SURVEY CAPABILITY NOW IN PRODUCTION

Ahurani Void, Alef Hestrixia Station — April 2026

The Nyridion Expansion Consortium [NEC] is pleased to announce the completion and maiden launch of its first Keystone-Class Strategic Mover — a purpose-built logistics and survey vessel representing years of focused research and manufacturing investment.

The Keystone-Class is designed for captains and factions who need to move material at scale and survey efficiently across established trade corridors. It is not a deep-range exploration vessel. It is a workhorse — built to keep your colonies supplied, your production lines fed, and your survey operations running on schedule.

PERFORMANCE SPECIFICATIONS

Every major system on the Keystone-Class has been manufactured from NEC's latest researched blueprints, delivering measurable gains over standard-spec equivalents:

- Reactor: AMX-L Core producing 3,242.78 MW (+53.78 MW over standard)
- Main Drive: MD-L delivering 130.73 MN of thrust (+10.73 MN over standard)
- Navigation: NavCom-L with 24.22s jump charge time (0.78s faster per jump)
- Scanner: RAK-L Material Scanner at scan level 103.19 (+13.19 over standard)
- Cargo: 5x improved Primary Cargo Hold expansions at 9,172 each (+1,172 per unit) — total cargo capacity of 395,860
- Fuel: 3x improved Primary Fuel Tank expansions at 2,294 each (+294 per unit) — total fuel capacity of 47,882

The numbers speak for themselves. Faster jumps, more cargo per run, better scan resolution on arrival. For captains running regular supply routes or survey sweeps, the Keystone-Class means fewer trips and better data.
