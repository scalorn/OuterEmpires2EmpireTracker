# Contacts

The Contacts form tracks the factions and external characters you interact with across the game. These contacts feed into combo lookups throughout the app — whenever you need to pick a counterparty, recipient, or faction, they come from here.

## Opening the Form

Open from **Manage → Contacts**.

## Factions Tab

The Factions tab lets you manage the player factions you deal with.

### Creating a Faction

1. Click **New** on the Factions tab.
2. Enter the **Faction Name**.
3. Optionally add **Notes** — alliance status, trade agreements, whatever you want to remember.
4. Click **Save**.

### Viewing Members

Select a faction to see its members listed below. This shows all external characters assigned to that faction, giving you a quick roster view.

### Reference Counting

The **Refs** column shows how many characters and player profiles reference each faction. Factions with active references can't be deleted — you'll need to reassign or remove those references first. This prevents orphaned data across the app.

## External Characters Tab

The External Characters tab tracks individual players and NPCs you interact with.

### Creating a Character

1. Click **New** on the External Characters tab.
2. Enter the **Character Name**.
3. Optionally assign a **Faction** from the dropdown.
4. Add any **Notes** you want to keep track of.
5. Click **Save**.

### Assigning to Factions

Each character can belong to one faction. Select a faction from the dropdown when creating or editing a character. If someone switches factions (it happens), just update the assignment.

## How Contacts Appear Across the App

Once you've added factions and characters, they show up in combo lookups throughout the tracker:

- **Recipient** fields on build items and delivery plans — who the item is for.
- **Counterparty** fields on market transactions — who you bought from or sold to.
- **Faction** filters on the Market Transactions tab — filter trades by faction.

This means you enter a contact once and it's available everywhere. No retyping names or worrying about spelling consistency.
