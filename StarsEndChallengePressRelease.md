# THE STARS END CHALLENGE

**Think you've got what it takes to reach the core of the galaxy?**

*Nyridion Expansion Consortium [NEC] - Bahzad Cluster, Stars End Outpost, Lystarix-Mu - May 2026*

---

## The Challenge

We built an outpost at the Galactic Core. Now we dare you to reach it.

After weeks of planning and a grueling expedition aboard our new Keystone-Class Strategic Mover, NEC has established **Stars End Outpost** deep in the Lystarix-Mu system - right at the edge of the galactic core.

To celebrate this achievement and encourage exploration across the galaxy, we're putting up a bounty for the first pilot brave enough to make the journey.

---

## The Prize: 3,000,000 Credits

Here's how it works:

1. **Travel to Stars End Outpost** in Lystarix-Mu (Bahzad Cluster)
2. **Purchase 1 share** in Stars End Outpost - listed at 1 credit
3. **Deliver that share** to **Scalorn Scorpus** at Alef Hestrixia Station in Ahurani Void

First pilot to complete all three steps collects **3 million credits**. No tricks, no lottery - just you, your ship, and a whole lot of empty space between here and there.

## Fair Warning

This is not a casual trip. The route to Lystarix-Mu crosses dozens of uncharted systems with no stations, no repairs, and no rescue. You will need:

- A ship with serious fuel range and very careful jump planning
- Enough credits to cover cloning if things go sideways
- Patience - this journey takes real time and real commitment

**NEC is not responsible for ship loss, cargo loss, piracy, navigation errors, fuel miscalculations, existential dread in deep space, or cloning costs incurred during your attempt.**

The Keystone-Class Strategic Mover was built for logistics. We wanted to see what it could do when pointed at the unknown. Turns out - it can go pretty far. Stars End Outpost is proof that NEC builds ships capable of reaching anywhere in the galaxy.

Now it's your turn to prove you can too.
