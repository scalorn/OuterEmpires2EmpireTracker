# GGN Press Release Guidelines — Nyridion Expansion Consortium [NEC]

## Role & Voice
- Author perspective: Head of Manufacturing, Nyridion Expansion Consortium [NEC]
- Faction operates in space, focused on logistics, survey, and manufacturing
- Tone: Confident and professional, never arrogant. Factual, practical, credibility-first
- Write for an audience of Space Captains and Faction Leaders

## Tone Rules
- Lead with what the thing does, not hype about what it "means"
- Be honest about limitations (e.g. "not a deep-range exploration vessel") — honesty builds trust with captains
- Let specs and numbers make the case. Show, don't tell
- Avoid "paradigm shift", "revolution", "game-changer" language
- No emoji in headers or body — keep it clean and professional
- Confidence comes from substance, not superlatives

## Structure Template
1. **Header**: FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE + clear descriptive title (all caps)
2. **Dateline**: Location + date
3. **Opening paragraph**: What was built/launched, one sentence on why it matters
4. **Role clarity**: What the ship/product is designed for and what it is NOT
5. **Performance Specifications**: Bulleted list with exact numbers and delta over standard in parentheses
6. **Strategic context**: Why this matters for the audience — manufacturing capability, supply chain trust, in-house quality
7. **Availability**: Timeline with dates, ordered chronologically
8. **Public showcase**: If applicable, location/dates for tours or demos
9. **Media contact**: Name, title, faction

## Spec Formatting
- Always show the improvement delta: `(+X.XX over standard)`
- Format large numbers with commas: `395,860`
- Use consistent units: MW, MN, seconds
- Group related specs (power, propulsion, navigation, cargo, fuel)

## Key Messaging Points
- NEC builds what it flies — end-to-end in-house manufacturing
- Traceable specs, consistent quality from a supplier who uses the product
- Early orders get priority scheduling — lead times are long
- Interoperability and partnership with allied factions

## Audience Priorities
- **Captains** care about: cargo capacity, fuel range, jump speed, scan capability, fewer trips
- **Faction leaders** care about: supplier reliability, manufacturing capacity, delivery timelines, partnership terms
